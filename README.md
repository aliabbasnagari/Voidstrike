# Voidstrike

Enter the endless depths of space in Voidstrike, an action-packed 2D space shooter where survival is your only mission. Take control of a powerful spaceship, battle waves of hostile enemies, dodge incoming attacks, and blast your way through the galaxy.

Fight to survive, sharpen your skills, and prove that you have what it takes to conquer the void!

Game tagline: "Strike through the darkness. Conquer the void."

Short description (for your project README):

Voidstrike is a 2D arcade-style space shooter game developed using the Godot Engine and GDScript. The player controls a spaceship, destroys incoming enemies, avoids enemy attacks, and tries to achieve the highest possible score.
